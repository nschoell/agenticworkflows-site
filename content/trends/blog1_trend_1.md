---
title: "Weekly AI Roundup: New Agentic Tools for Working Teams"
---

The pace of development in the agentic AI space is accelerating. This week, we're highlighting three tools that are making it easier for teams of every size to move beyond simple prompts and into full-scale automation.

### 1. LangGraph (by LangChain)
While often seen as a developer tool, LangGraph is fundamentally changing how "Agentic Workflows" are built. It allows for the creation of complex, stateful agents that can "loop" back to previous steps if they encounter an error. For a team, this means an agent that isn't just "one-and-done" but can actually troubleshoot a multi-step process (like a complex shipping calculation) before it fails.

### 2. CrewAI
CrewAI is gaining massive traction for its "multi-agent" approach. Instead of one agent doing everything, you can create a "crew" of specialized agents. One agent acts as the "Researcher," another as the "Writer," and a third as the "Manager" who checks the quality of the work. This mimics a real human team and is incredibly effective for content marketing and complex research projects.

### 3. Browse.ai
For those struggling with "data extraction," Browse.ai is a game-changer. It allows you to turn any website into a structured data feed without writing a scraper. When paired with a no-code tool like Zapier, it allows an agent to "watch" a competitor's pricing page or a news feed and automatically trigger a workflow whenever a change is detected.

**The Bottom Line:** The goal isn't to use the most complex tool; it's to find the one that solves your specific bottleneck. For most teams, starting with a single-agent workflow via Zapier or Make is the most effective way to begin.
