**Why it matters for managers**

Cyber‑threat detection is shifting from signature‑based rules to **AI‑generated context**. Instead of static alerts, security teams receive narratives that explain *how* an anomaly fits into broader attack patterns.

### Business impact
- **Faster response** – AI correlates logs across firewalls, endpoints, and cloud services, surfacing the most critical incidents within seconds.
- **Reduced fatigue** – By scoring alerts on business impact, the system suppresses low‑severity noise.
- **Strategic insight** – Weekly AI‑driven security briefs highlight emerging threat trends for leadership.

### Quick win checklist for security managers
1. Ingest logs from SIEM, firewalls, and endpoint agents into a **central vector store**.
2. Deploy a **local LLM** (e.g., `qwen3.8:27b-q8_0`) to summarize incidents.
3. Route AI‑generated alerts to a dedicated Slack channel.
4. Schedule a weekly digest for the C‑suite.

**Bottom line:** AI‑augmented security monitoring turns raw logs into actionable stories, helping managers prioritize and act faster.
